Made slowly, worn for years

UrbanWeave began in 2021 with a simple frustration: clothes that looked good often came at a cost to people and planet. We started with five pieces, one small workshop and a rule that every fabric must be traceable.

Today we work with 38 partner workshops, pay living wages and publish what each garment costs us to make. We design fewer pieces so each one earns its place in your wardrobe.

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Our team replies within one working day.

Contact us
How long does delivery take?

Orders ship within 2 working days. Peninsular Malaysia usually arrives in 2–4 days, East Malaysia and Singapore in 5–7 days.

What is your returns policy?

Return unworn items with tags within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Returns are free within Malaysia.

How do I find my size?

Use the size guide on any product page. Our pieces are cut true to size; if you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up for a relaxed fit.

How do I care for my garments?

Wash cold, line dry and skip the tumble dryer. Each product page lists specific care steps, and we offer free mending for any UrbanWeave piece.

Are your products really sustainable?

Every fabric is certified (GOTS, OEKO-TEX or Recycled Claim Standard) and we publish our supplier list and yearly impact report.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, FPX online banking, Touch 'n Go eWallet and GrabPay.
